Our Story

Dimple Star Transport traces its roots to Napat Transit, founded in 1993. In October of that year, our fleet — including bus NVR-963 (fleet No. 800) — could be spotted along Taft Avenue near United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila, navigating the busy thoroughfares alongside jeepneys under the Light Rail Line.

In May 2004, Napat Transit officially became Dimple Star Transport, embarking on a new chapter in Philippine public transportation. Today we proudly connect Metro Manila to the scenic province of Oriental Mindoro.
